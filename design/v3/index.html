Process

Concept, three directions, critique, refine.

This is the workflow from the saved post "How to stop making your apps look like AI slop". The generic answer has to compete with better ones before anything gets built.

01ConceptMake the AI's understanding visible and editable: shots, paths, light, pace.
02Three directionsSame screens, same components, three semantic mappings.
03DesignComponents first, tokens only. A template for every new screen.
04RenderHeadless renders at 390 × 844 for every screen and direction.
05CritiqueA checklist of generated-looking tells, and a script that enforces the rest.
06RefineFixes go into the system, not single screens.

A · Monitor Chosen

Graphite, periwinkle only for intelligence, white selection. The picture stays the hero and the AI's work reads at a glance. It has the strongest contrast and fewest competing colors.

B · Darkroom Explored

Warm umber blacks and a safelight accent, like a film lab. It has character, but warm chrome tints every photo's judgment, and the safelight orange collides with the caution and warm-light signals.

C · Lumen Explored

Paper ground and ink type. It's friendly and clear for settings and plans, but bright chrome fights footage in the camera and editor. Rendering it exposed a real bug: text over photos inherited dark ink. That bug produced the new on-media tokens.

Agent skill

mova-design: the system, packaged for agents.

Made with skill-creator. It triggers on any MOVA screen, state, onboarding, App Store or motion request, and carries the tokens, components, motion layer, a screen template, the process, and a checker that enforces the rules.
