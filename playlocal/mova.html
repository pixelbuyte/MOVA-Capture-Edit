MOVA · camera & creative studio

Show it a video. Make it yours.

MOVA is a professional iPhone camera and editor. Give it a reference video and it learns how that video was made, scene by scene, then builds a new one from your own photo or footage.

What it is

Three tools that work as one.

01 · CAPTURE

A real pro camera

Manual focus, exposure and white balance, RAW, histogram, zebras and Log where your iPhone supports it. Free, with no credits needed.

02 · RECREATE

Reference to your version

Camera moves, light, pacing and transitions from a video you love, rebuilt around your own media.

03 · EDIT

Finish it properly

A multitrack timeline with captions, color and audio, plus an assistant that suggests changes you can preview first.

How recreation works

Six steps from reference to your film.

  1. 1
    Import a referenceFrom Photos, Files or your MOVA Library.
  2. 2
    MOVA analyzes itShots, camera moves, lighting, grade, transitions and pacing.
  3. 3
    Add your mediaA selfie, a pet, a product, a landscape or your own footage.
  4. 4
    Choose what carries overKeep the moves, change the light, and direct the rest in plain words.
  5. 5
    See the cost, then generateDuration, aspect, resolution and credits are shown before you tap.
  6. 6
    Edit, save or exportA new video, not a filter over the original.
How we build it

The same honest rules.

  1. 01

    Capture is free

    The camera and editor never need credits or a subscription.

  2. 02

    Costs up front

    Every AI action shows its credit cost before you start, and failed generations are refunded.

  3. 03

    No fake progress

    Real stages and real bytes, never invented percentages. Unsupported camera features are shown as unavailable.
